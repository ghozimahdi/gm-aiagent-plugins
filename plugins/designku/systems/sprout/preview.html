Overview

Design rationale

Who this system is for and the reasoning behind each decision. Agents must use these tokens and components — never guess a color, font, spacing or radius.

Tokens→Atoms→Molecules→Organisms→Templates→Pages

Audience

Children 6-12 with a parent nearby, and casual adult learners doing five-minute sessions; developing readers and imprecise motor control.

Why it looks like this

Color

Leaf green signals growth and 'correct', and is gender-neutral for children. A softer leaf-green secondary keeps selection and tonal states in the brand family, and a sunshine yellow tertiary supplies the playful accent for stars, streaks and rewards. Because green doubles as success, wrong answers use an error container with an icon and a gentle message rather than a red screen.

Typography

Baloo 2 headlines are chunky and friendly. Nunito body has rounded terminals and clearly distinct letterforms (a, g, l, I), which helps early readers.

Shape and density

Rounded 16-24px corners and spacious density: 52px buttons and 72px list rows forgive imprecise taps.

Motion

Expressive: 120% durations and an overshoot curve on the emphasized easing make rewards feel alive. Always respect reduce-motion.

Do / Don't

  • Celebrate progress with tertiary (stars, streaks) and primary (correct, continue).
  • Use icons plus words on every button; never rely on reading alone or color alone.
  • Keep one task per screen with a large primary button anchored at the bottom.
  • Do not punish with red full-screen states; use errorContainer and an encouraging retry.
  • Do not go below bodyLarge for instructions aimed at children.
  • Do not place destructive or purchase actions near the primary button.
