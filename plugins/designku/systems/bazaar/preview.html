Overview

Design rationale

Who this system is for and the reasoning behind each decision. Agents must use these tokens and components — never guess a color, font, spacing or radius.

Tokens→Atoms→Molecules→Organisms→Templates→Pages

Audience

Deal-seeking shoppers browsing fast with one thumb, comparing many items, often on mid-range Android devices.

Why it looks like this

Color

A hot orange-red primary draws the eye to Buy and Add-to-cart; warm hues raise arousal and are the convention shoppers already know from major marketplaces. It is darkened to tone 45 so white button labels pass AA (most marketplace oranges do not). A deep navy secondary anchors checkout and payment screens with trust, and a bright yellow tertiary is reserved for promos and ratings. Error is shifted to crimson so it can never be confused with the orange CTA.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans throughout: friendly, compact, and its bold weights keep prices punchy at small sizes.

Shape and density

Soft 8-12px corners maximize the image area on product cards. Comfortable density keeps the buy button a full 48px.

Motion

Snappy (80% durations) — browsing should feel fast; add-to-cart feedback is immediate.

Do / Don't

  • Keep exactly one filled primary button visible in the sticky bottom bar (Add to cart / Buy now).
  • Show price in titleLarge with strong weight; strike-through prices use onSurfaceVariant.
  • Use tertiaryContainer for promo chips and star ratings.
  • Do not use primary for discounts and errors at the same time — discount badges use tertiary, failures use error.
  • Do not put primary-colored text on surfaceContainerHigh/Highest (contrast drops below AA for text).
  • Do not animate every card; motion budget goes to cart and checkout feedback.
